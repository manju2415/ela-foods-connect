**Ela Bazar live functional test report — 8 September 2026**

Tested https://elabazar.com with the five phone-login accounts supplied in the spreadsheet, plus isolated guest sessions. Desktop viewport: 1440 × 900. Mobile viewport: 390 × 844, Chromium mobile emulation. This extends the earlier public-page audit. Findings describe the deployment observed during testing; concurrent local edits were not modified or deployed by this audit.

**Confirmed failures and access inconsistencies**

| Priority | Finding | Reproduction and observed result | Recommended correction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Critical | Fixed test credentials grant production super-admin access | Sign in through `/auth` using the supplied super-admin test account and fixed test OTP. The real admin dashboard, user list with 112 users, settings, founder records, and payment-recovery pages are accessible. The deployed public JavaScript bundle also contains the privileged test phone list and fixed OTP. No admin records were changed. | Remove the fixed-OTP bypass from production server authentication, remove production privileges from QA accounts, invalidate affected sessions, and keep test authentication confined to an isolated environment. Hiding the frontend constants alone is insufficient. |
| High | Refreshing checkout abandons the checkout flow | With the supplied customer account's existing cart, click Proceed to Checkout. `/checkout` shows one item, subtotal ₹70, delivery ₹75, total ₹145. Reload the page: it redirects to `/products` even though the cart still contains the item. Direct initial navigation also redirects. | Wait for authentication and cart loading to finish before applying empty-cart redirects. Preserve checkout navigation during initial data loading. |
| High | Rapid quantity clicks are lost | In a fresh guest cart, add the 12-inch and 16-inch Floor Wiper variants. With the 12-inch quantity settled at 1, click its plus control five times quickly, then wait five seconds. Its final quantity is 3, not 6; the other variant remains at 1. | Apply increments against current state and serialize or otherwise coordinate asynchronous cart updates. Prevent stale responses from replacing newer quantities. |
| High / permission review | Network manager cannot see the full user dataset | Supplied network-admin account: `/mlm-admin/users` shows only its own account, “All Users (1)”. Super admin's user screen shows 112. Network dashboard reports one member. | Medium | Network-admin menu links to a forbidden page | Signed-in network admin opens the visible Founding Members menu destination, `/mlm-admin/founding-members`. | Medium | Customer Wishlist and Settings links lead to membership payment | In `/customer-app/profile`, click Wishlist or Settings. The first public-page audit also found a Banana Chips weight discrepancy (175g listing/cart versus 140g details), placeholder social links, incorrect Explore Categories destination, a missing sitemap, a banner 406 response, and policy-content issues. The Floor Wiper prices were confirmed to represent different variants: 12-inch member price ₹165 versus 16-inch member price ₹190. Variant pricing itself works; the homepage needs to identify the variant or label its price “From”.

**Limits and follow-up needed**

This is a broad live functional audit, not an end-to-end transaction certification. No real payments, orders, wallet credits/debits, refunds, withdrawals, delivery-status changes, registration fees, account deletion, OTP SMS, contact messages, or newsletter submissions were initiated. Production settings and application code were not changed. Login itself can update the test account's normal authentication/session metadata. Guest cart edits used isolated browser sessions; the existing customer cart was inspected without changing its items.

The spreadsheet labels one account “Member”, but the live profile identifies it as Regular Customer and redirects member home to registration payment. Paid-member pricing, commission allocation, referrals after paid registration, wallet-funded purchases, successful/failed/cancelled payment callbacks, stock deduction after payment, and refund restoration still need an activated test member and a controlled transaction environment. Native Android/iOS behavior, real devices, cross-browser coverage, load/concurrency testing, and full accessibility testing are also outstanding. Client-side stock limits do not establish server-side stock enforcement.

Payment recovery currently flags three unfinished registration payments; this is an operational follow-up item, not proof from this audit that customers were charged incorrectly. Existing pending/cancelled orders were inspected, not reconciled or changed.
